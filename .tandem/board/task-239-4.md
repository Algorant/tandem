## Description

This is a joint design session, not autonomous work. Do not delegate, and do not implement any part of task-239 until this closes with a recorded decision.

Scope was widened to absorb task-228's open questions. Both tasks define what happens when work leaves active state: this one decides whether work stops at `validation`, task-228 defines what cleanup runs when it stops. Answering them separately risks two different answers to "who completes work and when."

## Open questions: validation state

- Should `validation` remain a workflow state at all, or become a flag/reason on a task that is otherwise in `in-progress`?
- Does accord `accepted` imply completion, or stay separate from workflow state entirely?
- Is a validation reason required? If so, what is the closed vocabulary, and is it enforced as an error or a warning?
- Who may complete a task directly from `in-progress`: any orchestrator, or only under stated evidence conditions?
- What counts as sufficient objective evidence for the orchestrator to complete without human review?
- Is there a target ratio or audit mechanism for how much work legitimately sits in validation?
- How do existing tasks currently parked in `validation` get reconciled?
- Is `review.status` the right audit hook? It warned `missing` on both task-237 and task-238 completions and was never set by any step in the flow.

## Open questions: acceptance and archival boundary (from task-228)

- What is the trigger for tidy-up, given the answer to the questions above? task-228 currently assumes "when it moves from `validation` to accepted," which becomes invalid if `validation` changes shape.
- Should the tidy-up commit be separate from the work commit, or folded into it?
- Should tidy-up be automated later, or stay a documented manual checklist in v0?
- Who commits `.tandem/` state, and when? See papercut-5: Worker-side `tandem accord` writes land uncommitted in the Worker's worktree and are destroyed at cleanup, so the orchestrator currently records all transitions from the main checkout.

## Required inputs

- `papercut-4`: integration completes without verifying board state.
- `papercut-5`: Worker-side accord transitions do not survive integration.
- The task-237/task-238 evidence recorded in task-239.

## Output

One `tandem decision` record capturing the agreed rules for both validation and the acceptance/archival boundary. task-239-1, task-239-2, task-239-3, and task-228 execute that decision and must not restate or reinvent it.
