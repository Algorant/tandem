## Description

This is a joint design session, not autonomous work. Do not delegate, and do not implement any part of task-239 until this closes with a recorded decision.

Open questions to settle with the human:

- Should `validation` remain a workflow state at all, or become a flag/reason on a task that is otherwise in `in-progress`?
- Does accord `accepted` imply completion, or stay separate from workflow state entirely?
- Is a validation reason required? If so, what is the closed vocabulary, and is it enforced as an error or a warning?
- Who may complete a task directly from `in-progress`: any orchestrator, or only under stated evidence conditions?
- What counts as sufficient objective evidence for the orchestrator to complete without human review?
- Is there a target ratio or audit mechanism for how much work legitimately sits in validation?
- How do existing tasks currently parked in `validation` get reconciled?

Output: a `tandem decision` record capturing the agreed rules. Subtasks task-239-1, task-239-2, and task-239-3 execute that decision and must not restate or reinvent it.
